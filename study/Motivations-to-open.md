### As a vessel of mercy:

- we are in awe of His mercy, by which He loved us, choosing us before the foundation of the world according to His foreknowledge, by which He knew our entire future condition and still approved us, chose us, came in time to die us and possess us, and will one day come back to marry us. This motivates us to love Him and treasure Him.

### As a vessel of honor:

#### as a teacher

we are motivated to be those nourished with the healthy words of life, that we would have a portion to teach others. (??)

#### as a solider

- because of the different teachings, we are motivated to be equiped with the truth to stand against the decline of the church and fight for the truth for the sake of the Body

- because of the severe attack of death from Satan against the church, we are motivated to be full of life (to be in ascension) to reign in life over sin, Satan and death.

#### as an athlete

because of the desire to win the reward of the crown of righteousness, we are motivated to practice our skill of remaining open to the Lord, that we would run lawfully within the lanes, by fleeing into Christ as our refuge from all negative things,  and pursuing, even persecuting, Christ, that we may lay hold of Him as our reward.

#### as a farmer

who desires to scatter the seed and water the crop, we are motivated to love Him and pursue Him in His Word that we would have an aura emanating out from us that is the real attraction, and penetrating potent power, when we speak or even just when we go about our daily life.

#### as a workman

who desires to cut straight the word of the truth, we are motivated to be simple, not complicated, but remaining in the enjoyment and impartation of life in the Word, that we would live out a demonstration of the truth we enjoy which will innoculate, enlighten and recover the people witnessing our living.

### As a vessel of glory:

- for the sake of our ministry we are motivated to "face the face" and "treasure the treasure" by remaining in the presence of the Lord all the time, so that we would be filled with His glory, even abounding in such glory, that we could reflect Him, beam Him, and shine Him forth into others.

### As a grain of wheat:

- our failures, mistakes, wrong excretions, etc, motivate us to not want to live by our natural man

- the outward consuming pressures motivate us to (flee?) turn from our natural man to our inner man, that the outward man may be consumed and our inner man may be renewed to become our new person
