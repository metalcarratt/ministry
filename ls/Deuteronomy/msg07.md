enter into marriage allaince with them, and tear down all their idols.

but these nations were devilish and had been mingled with demons. therefore
they had to be utterly destroyed.

and take Christ. then we will have God's blessing of love, peace, fruitfulness
and health.

need to keep "the whole Christ" so that the church might have longevity and multiplication.

remembering God's humbling of us and feeding us manna so as to learn that man lives not by bread alone but by every word tbat proceeds out of the mouth of Jehovah. the Lord disciplines us out of His love.

keeping His ways.
