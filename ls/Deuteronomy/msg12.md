deut 13. concerning apostasy. which in the OT means giving up God and turning to
idols and in the NT means denying the diety of Christ. deut 12 is specific about
keeping the oneness, deut 13 specific about keeping away from apostasy. in the NT
paul was very focused on caring for the oneness while John was focused on keeping
away from apostasy.

teaching apostasy is different from being wrong in doctrine. to keep the oneness
we should be general to receive all believers, regardless of their doctrinal
incorrectness. for example, paul says some who are weaker in faith keep days
or abstains from foods, but that we should receive them. however he is strong that
we should mark those who create divisions and turn away from them.

similarily john says if one does not confess Jesus as the Christ to not greet or
talk to them.

moses says:
- put to death the dreamer of dreams who advocates apostasy
- slay the brother, son, slave, daughter, wife or friend who secretly entices to serve other gods
- destroy the whole city when those in that city had gone to serve other gods.
