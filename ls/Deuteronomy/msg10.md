deut 12 - the way to worship God

worshipping God only in the place of His choice, the place of His habitation. not
having our own choice.

to utterlt destroy everything to do with idols, destroying their name from those places.
this is the opposite of what Catholicism has done, bringing in many ways of worship
from the nations.

four points: 
- God's people should always be one, with onky one worship center
- gathered to the place that has His name; we shouod bear His name only
- to the place of His habitation. today this is our spirit, and we must reject the flesh, the self
- the altar, the cross; at the entrance to the church is the cross, and everyone must experience it.
