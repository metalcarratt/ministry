to John 1, He is also the Word. As the Word, and as the life-giving Spirit, He is now
near us, in our mouth and in our heart. As such He is our food. He as the Spirit and

#### the words of moses as the spokesman of God and an aged, loving father

the book of deuteronomy is mostly Moses' speaking, and as one who was directly in God's presence for such a long time (40 years), and in the principle of Paul's word "but i think i also have the Spirit of Christ", we can consider this as God's speaking. thus this can be considered the longest discourse of God's speaking in the Bible.

Moses spoke as a loving father to his children with much love and concern.
