deaut 30:11 speaks of the commandment.
vv 12-13 talk of "it" not being in heaven or across the sea.
v 14 says the "word" is near you, in your mouth and heart.

Christ is the One who came down from heaven and who arose from the Abyss. According
to John 1, He is also the Word. As the Word, and as the life-giving Spirit, He is now
near us, in our mouth and in our heart. As such He is our food. He as the Spirit and
the Word is our food to be our life and life supply.

From this we see that every word in Deuteronomy is Christ.
