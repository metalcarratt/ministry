the book of deut does not specifically mention Christ or the Spirit but there
are synonyms of Christ. these are the laws, commandments, testimonies, statutes,
and judgments (ordinances). because they are all spoken by God they are the Word
of God. 

In Rom 10 Paul interprets the word in Deut as Christ. to keep the law is
to keep Christ. to love the commandments is to love Christ. Paul's intepretation
in Rom 10 also implies the Spirit. the word is "near you, in your heart and in
your mouth." Paul presents Christ as the Word who was incarnated, crucified and
resurected and now is in our heart and mouth. to be in our heart and mouth Christ
had to go through a process to become the Spirit.
