God required the children of Israel to come to a physical place three times a
year to preserve the oneness. according to their history this was the thing that
preserved the oneness.

what is the fulfillment today? in eph 2:22 Paul says we are being built together into
a dwelling place of God in spirit. in John 4 the Lord told the samaritan woman that
the time is coming when those who worship the Father will worship Him in spirit. so
the location today must be our spirit.

we meet in spirit, and we have the cross (the altar), yet we cannot physically all
meet in the same place, so we have different localities. but we are still one, only separated by geography. christianity is not like this. they have their preferences
and are not willing to come together even if they are in the same place. they may
profess oneness but cannot even pray together if they meet, for they all have thier
own way of praying.
