deuteronomy reveals Christ as our goal, the good land. it also reveals Christ
as our way to reach the land, the Word.

the expressions _law_, _commandments_, _statutes_, _ordinances_, and _judgments_ are
all synonyms for Christ as the Word, and the way we receive Christ, who is now
the Spirit, is by inhaling His Word.

our living, acting, behaving should be not I but Christ by inhaling Christ as
the living Word.
