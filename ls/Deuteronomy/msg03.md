the main points of deuteronomy reveal three people:

God is loving, righteous, faithful and blessing:
- His heart is loving
- His hands (doings) are righteous
- His word is faithful
- His eyes will either bless or curse

man is nothing. we love ourselves and are righteous for our own benefit.

Christ as the Word who can be and do everything for us. God's goal is not that we
would do something but take Christ. the whole Bible is not a book of stories, it is
Christ as our food and breath. everything of Christ is embodied in the word as the
Bible.

#### review of the past
deuteronomy shows the need to have a review of the past. this should not be
a review of retrospection, but with an exercised Spirit, and under the governing
thought that:
- God's beart is love and his dealing is righteous
- God blesses our obedience and faithfulness
- turning our heart away from God results in tragedy

the review covers:
- at the mount of God, receiving the revelation of the law, the ark, the tabernacle, the building, thd priesthood, and being formed into an army
- being led by God
- failing God at kadesh barnea because of their unbelief
- wandering for 38 years
- slaying Sihon and Og, the guards to the entrance of the good land.

unbelief equals disobedience to God. the NT is a ministry and word of faith. unbelief damages us and leads to tragedy.

> With respect to believing in God, we should forget out past, but with respect to knowing God and ourselves we should remember our past.
