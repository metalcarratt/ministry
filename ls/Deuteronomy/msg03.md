the main points of deuteronomy reveal three people:

God is loving, righteous, faithful and blessing:
- His heart is loving
- His hands (doings) are righteous
- His word is faithful
- His eyes will either bless or curse

man is nothing. we love ourselves and are righteous for our own benefit.

Christ as the Word who can be and do everything for us. God's goal is not that we
would do something but take Christ. the whole Bible is not a book of stories, it is
Christ as our food and breath. everything of Christ is embodied in the word as the
Bible.
