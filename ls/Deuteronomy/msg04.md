the time tbe cbildren of Israel spent in the wilderness for 38 years had some
positive purposes:
- to consume man's flesh and unbelief
- to manifest God's mercy and blessing (in spite of their failures)
- to consume all the unbelieving ones
- to produce a new generation

God was the One who defeated the kings Sihin and Og, and this was a guarantee
that God would do the same in all the good land where God would bring them in.

God did not allow Moses to enter the land because he violated God's authority. this was a warning to the children of Israel not to treat Gods government lightly, and to know God loves us not to spoil us but to perfect us.
