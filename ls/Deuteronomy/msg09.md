the blessings for keeping the whole commandment, of loving Gos:
- being strong to possess Christ
- extending our days in Christ, a land Jehovah cares for, seeks after, has His eyes on continually
- rain in its season, the Spirit
- blessed with loving God as a woman loves a man, by putting God's word (Christ) on their heart and soul, wearing them in front of their eyes, teaching it to their children, speaking about it in their house, on the way, when they lie down and when they rise up.
- dispossessing all the nations before them

the curse for seeking after other gods, whom we have not known, is to lose God's care.

the blessing was to be placed on one mountain, Mount Gerizim, as a reminder, and the
curse on another mountain, Mount Ebal.
