one of the most difficult verses in revelation is 3:12, the promise that we would
be made into a pillar in the temple of God and have the name of Jesus, God and the New
Jerusalem written on us.

this is the greatest blessing. it is not what God will give us, but ehat He will
make us. these verse implies both transformation and building. (to not go out anymore
is a matter of building, a matter of dependence)

> In the church life we should not expect objective blessings. Rather, we must realise that the Lord's blessing is always to make us something, to transform us into precious material, and then to build us into God's building. If you take this word into you, it will absolutely revolutionize your concept.


> I am deeply burdened! It does not matter whether you are good or bad, holy or unholy, spiritual or unspiritual. The only thing that matters is whether or not you have been touched by the Lord and transformed. We must be willing to open ourselves and say, "Lord, get through. Lord flow in, through, and out of me and carry away every particle of my natural element. Lord I hate the outward improvement and am tired of being corrected outwardly. I am tired of religion and Bible knowledge. I am tired of being individually spiritual. Lord, I am desperate about my condition because I have so little of Your divine eseence. I have been tsught and edified for years. But still I am what I am."
