#### the late overcomers

the late overcomers are those who overcome by being slain by the beast. these stand
on a glassy sea, the consummation of God's judgment, indicating they have passed above
all judgment. they have harps of God and sing the song of Moses and the Lamb. the
song of Moses is the song of victory over Pharaoh, and the song of the Lamb is the
song of His redemption.

the late overcomers praise God for His acts, which are mighty, and His ways, which are
righteous. His ways are His governing principles. even though at this time God has
not yet avenged their deaths, they know that God will, because they know His ways.

#### tha ark in the temole seen

in the first half of the book of revelation the throne with the rainbow is the center. this section is on judgment. because of the rainbow, even though God judges the earth, He does so within a limitation, that those of the sheep nations may be spared. in the second half of the book of revelation the ark of the covenant within the holy of holies in the tabernacle is the center. this section is on God's building.

#### the pouring out of the seven bowls

the angels who come to pour out the bowls are dressed in priestly robes, not military uniform. this indicates that their office is fulfilling the prayers of the late overcomers. these overcomers did not pray for vengence, rather they praised God. 

these angels come out from the temple, from the throne. this shows the mingling of judgment with expression. the purpose of judgment is to gain expression. this is true also in our spiritual life. the continuation of this chapter, 16, is 19, the coming of the bride (17 and 18 are an insertion concerning babylon). this further reinforces the thought that judgment is for God's expression, His testimony.

the fact God's fury is in bowls shows that even in pouring out His wrath, God is living. however the fact that the smoke fills the temple and no one can enter into it until the bowls have been poured out indicate that once God has begun to pour out His wrath He will not be interceeded of by anyone until He is finished.
