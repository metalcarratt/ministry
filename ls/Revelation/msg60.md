God's building is not a physical city. the matter of the stone is all throughout
the Bible, especially precious stones. The Lord told Peter that he was a stone and
that the revelation of Christ is the rock upon which His house is built. Peter also
said that we are living stones. therefore to be a stone is to be a transformed
person, and to be the building, the city of God, is to be built up.

the gates of the city have the names of the twelve tribes of Israel. firstly the
gospel came out through the apostles, whom were all Jews. then the Gentiles came
in to the city through them.

the gates are of the twelve tribes of israel, but the foundations of the wall are
the apostles.
