the lampstand. the lampstand is first seen in exodus, developed in zechariah, and further
developed in revelation. after putting all these together we see four important matters
that we must know and experience: the element, the form, the expression or shining,
and the reproduction.

the element is God as gold. to form a lampstand we need not a little gold but a lump
of gold. we need one talent. we gain gold by coming to God every day, even throughout
the day, remaining open to Him and exercising our spirit and asking Him to fill us.

the lampstand was not a lump of gold but beaten together into a shape. the members may have their individual portions of Christ, their gold, but as the cburch they need to be beaten together. this is to lose our individuality and be built together as one entity.
