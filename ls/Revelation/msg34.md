the universal bright woman. she is clothed with the sun, standing on the moon,
and wears a crown of twelve stars. the stars signify the patriarchs before the
law was given; the moon signifies the nation of Israel; the part clothed with the
sun signifies the church, since the day has come with the coming of Christ.

we are all women in the sight of God.

to understand rev 12 we must see Gen 3. in Gen 3 there is the woman, the serpent,
and the seed of the woman. in rev 12 is the larger woman. the dragon, and the
manchild.
