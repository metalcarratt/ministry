the seventh trumpet

consisting of the seven bowls, the eternal kingdom, the judgment of the dead, the
reward to the saints and the God-fearing one who pass through the great tribulaton,
and the destruction of the destroyers of the earth.

all the believers are resurrected and judged by Christ in the air prior to the
battle at armageddon. paul also mentions the judging of the believers during the
"last trumpet" in 1 corinthians.

this is the end of the first section of revelation, and at the end we see the temple
in heaven opened and the ark seen. the first half of revelation concerns God's
throne, and the judgment which issues forth from it. the second half of revelation
concerns the temple of God as the consummation of all God's positive work in the
universe to gain an expression for Himself.
