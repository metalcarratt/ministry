the firstfruits

- 144,000 being 12 x 12 x 1000. 12 is 4x3, the mingling of divinity with humanity and the consummation of Gods work in eternity.
- they sing a new song, having the sound like many waters. this is the loudness with which they praise the Lord
- not defiled with women. this is their keeping their virginity in relation to the Lord, their attitude to not do certain things the Lord does to allow then to do, not because of fear but because of their love for the Lord,
