Christ as their head.

> The Lord has shown us the most effective way to defeat the enemy. What we need to do is take the covenant, the Word of God, and preach to Satan regarding what the Lord has accomplished for us. For example,  whenever we were ruled by our temper in the past, we firstly confessed it, then asked the Lord for forgiveness, prayed for the Lord to help us, and made up our mind never to lose our temper again. I experienced this many times. But we should not do this anymore. When our temper is aroused, we should forget it and preach to Satan. Go to the source of the problem--Satan--and prrach to him. saying, "Satan, you have been crucified on the cross." The more you preach to Satan, the more you will be released.

today we need to speak like this to Satan. then, as the manchild, we will be raptured to heaven when we see Satan there we will give him another message, telling him to get out. after that we will dewcend to the earth and tell him to go to the abyss. after one thousand years we will give our fourth message to him and tell him to go to the lake of fire.

do not be indifferent. 

> The longer you make your message, the better it will be. Also, it is better for you to do this preaching individually. If you preach to Satan like this, you will become another person and will probably soon be included in the stronger part of the woman.
