the war at armageddon.

here, Christ is shown as:
- riding a white horse, pure and bright, in the gospel
- faithful and true
- judging and fighting in righteousness
- with eyes like a flame of fire
- with many diadems (glories)
- having. name written known only to Himself (showing His inexhaustableness)
- with a garment dipped in the blood if His enemies.
- being the Word of God, not only for life and building, as in the gosepl of John. but also for fighting
- having a sharp sword proceeding out of the mouth, showing that He fights by speaking in His authority
- shepherding the nations with an iron rod, because of the rebellious nature within man, the nations that are not smitten will need to be shepherded with an iron rod
- treading the winepress of the wrath of God
- having tbe nane "king of kings and lord of lords" written in His garment and thigh
