the rapture of the believers is of two aspects: the overcomers and the majority of
the believers. this is the picture of the harvest of the firstfruits vs the crop. the
firstfruits were taken early, to the house. while the harvest was taken to the barn,
between the field and the house.

the need of the rapture is because of the coming of tbe great tribulation, which
will be a snare (a trap laid to capture an animal) laid by Satan. regarding this,
we should not be snared by the things of this life. but we do still need to do
our duty and work hard. we also need to be raptured because at that time the devil
will come down to the earth with great wrath and make war with the woman and her seed.
there will also be the beast, antichrist, making war with the saints and overcoming
them.

the reward of the rapture is to escape all these things to stand before the son
of man, to escape the hour of trial.
