the harvest of the believers - in the typology of the harvest there are three stages
of the harvest: the firstfruits, the harvest, and the gleanings (Lev. 23:22). after
the harvest of the firstfruits is the harvest of the majority of the believers. at
this time the Lord will be on the cloud (made public) and will send forth His sickle
(the angels) to reap the earth. In Rev 16:15 the Lord gives a warning close to the
seventh bowl. this is after the harvest meaning it is to the gleaning of those
still not raptured.
