the trumpeting of the seventh trumpet.

Christ comes and "another strong Angel". He is clothed with the cloud
(still His secret coming), but puts his foot on the land and sea,
indicating His possession of it. He cries with a loud voice like a
lion, the seven thunders utter their voices. He declares there will
be delay no longer. 

this trumpet is the ending of mysteries. Christ has a small opened
scroll in His hand, which is what remains after the rest of the scroll
is unveiled. John takes and devours it. it is sweet in his mouth
but bitter in his stomach. we all must take the revelation in this
way, by devouring it, but know that the working out of this revelation in
our being will cause some suffering.
