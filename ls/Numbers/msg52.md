the cbildren of Israel passed through many trials and frustration for forty years
in the wilderness, yet except Joshua and Caleb, the ones who enetered the good land
were the younger ones. there was a passing on of the lessons learned by the older
ones to the younger ones, so that they might be prepared to enter the good land.

the young ones received an inheritance from the ones who went before them,
being trained and prepared to enter the good land by:
- being saved from Egypt by the saving power of the entire Triune God
- enjoying the heavenly provision of manna, flowing water, and God's care as a nursing mother.
- receiving the knowledge and training to know God, to be built together, and to carry out the priesthood
- being formed into an army
- passing through frustrations, trials and disciplines to be purged (by fire) and purified (by water)
- receiving the pre arrangement of the distribution of the land
