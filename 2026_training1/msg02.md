# Message Two - The Eternal Life--The life of God with the Divine Nature, the Triune God being the structure of the Bible, and the hidden God, a God who hides Himself

God is hidden. He is with us all the time. He knows our situation. But we will get there at the right time.

## I. The divine life may be considerd as the first and the basic attribute of God.

God's life is the unique life. God is, and He just is. There is no source of God. He is the beginning.

## II. The eternal life is the life of God with the divine nature

### A. The eternal life

We have eternal life in this age. Given by the Lord, when we believed.

Life is also a reward in the coming age, to those who give up things for the Lord's sake, to those who are faithful unto death, and to those who overcome.

We will enjoy life in eternity as the water of life and tree of life.

How much do we live by the tree of life? For so many saints in their daily living its all about right and wrong. Not, is this the divine life or not? Just come to the tree of life and receive His flowing.

### B. The divine nature

Partakers of the divine nature. We have a human nature, but are partaking of the divine nature. How can I, a human being, experience divine nature? Christ had divine life, became a human being to have human life. Now we have the human life and were born again to have the divine life. Now the Triune God is making His divine nature known to us, gradually, going deeper and deeper into our being.

"Lord, I'm not holding anything back. You have access to the depths of my being. I don't even know the depths of my being. I'm giving you access to every part of my being."

We are not empty in any place. So when he goes into the depths of our being He has to remove many things. It could be your imaginations, your memories. This is His shepherding. He is marvelous at it. Do you have a personal relationship with the Lord? Chose a particular part of the New Testament, and for 5-6 minutes every day just pray-read. Over time, how much Christ will you take into you? It's not quick, but it's enduring.

"a rich entrance" into the kingdom. Not just making it somehow, not passing some examination, but having a rich entrance. Aren't we willing to go through all sorts of things to be able to stand before Him in that day and hear Him say such a word to us?

2 Pet 1:4, another nature imparted into us.

partake, enjoy, participate.

pray about point 6. that it would not just be an objective point, but a description of your being and what your are enjoying day by day. "If we enjoy God and partake of the riches of His being, we will be constituted with the divine nature, becoming the same as God in life and nature but not in the Godhead and expressing Him in all that we are and do."

## III. The Triune God is the structure of the New Testament

A. The Lord, by the Spirit, cast our the demons (Matt12:28)
B. Baptize the nations into the name (Matt28:19)
C. The Father sending the Spirit in the name of the Son (John14:26)
D. The Spirit of reality proceeds from the Father and testifies concerning the Son (John15:26)
E. The Lord exalted to the Father and receiving the promise of the Holy Spirit (Acts2:33)
F. The Spirit of the Father raised the Lord, and He will also give life to our bodies through the Spirit indwelling us. (R8:11)

When there is pain and suffering in our bodies, sometimes the Lord would give young believers a gift of healing. But many of us realise the Lord is allowing us to be alive but not focusing on our body. We have to accept that. We have to be an ordinary human being regarding this. But there will be times when our mortal body receives resurrection life dispensed into us. He's not healing us, but He's saturating us. There may be some saints enjoying resurrection life, just what (just enough) they need for their human living.

G. The Father sent the Spirit of the Son into our hearts crying Abba Father (Gal4:6)

We need to have fellowship with the Father, even personally. Then at the table meeting we can praise the Father for being the source of everything. "I love You, God, my Father."

H. The Father granting us through the Spirit to be strengthened to the inner man that the Lord would make home in our hearts(Eph3:16-17)
I. One Spirit, one Lord, one God and Father (Eph4:4-6)
J. Christ offering His blood through the Spirit to God (Heb9:14)
K. foreknowledge of the Father, sanctification of the Spirit, blood of Jesus Christ (1P1:2)
L. Him who is, was and is coming, the seven Spirits, and Jesus Christ (Rev1:4-5)

## IV. God is a God who hides Himself - the hidden God

God created everything and then hid Himself. He is hiding but He is active, in a very precious, mysterious way.

"Surely You are a God who hides Himself, O God of Israel the Saviour" (Isa45:15)

He might say to us, "Although you can't see me, and most of the time can't feel me, I'm taking care of your inward being in every moment and in every situation."

In the book of Esther God is not mentioned, but He is doing many things in secret, in a hidden way. He is doing many things, especially through this one sister. The grandfather was the source of understanding. We can see how the Lord was the hiding God, saving millions of the people and defeated the enemy.

God created the universe and then hid Himself in it, until we do not know where to find Him. He created you and then He hid. But He knew the time would come we would want to seek after Him, know Him, experience Him. 

The parable of the widow and the judge is profound. We need to know God as He is revealed, as the hidden God.
- The believers are a widow because their husband Christ is "apparantly" absent from them.
- pray persistently and don't lose heart. many have the experience of praying for something or someone, for months, years, maybe 20 years. they may want to give up but something in them (their being) compels them to keep praying. In His time, in His way, He will give the answer. Go on, keep praying, until the Lord manifests Himself. He is still hiding, but He is receiving your prayers. In ways you don't know, He is functioning it. In His way and at His time it will be revealed to you. The enemy will be put to shame and God will be glorified and you will be matured in the divine life.
- God "temporarily" "appears" to be unrighteous, because He does not deal with His enemy straight away
- Even though He appears to be unrighteous, we must bother Him again and again. Bother Him with your praying. Our bothering Him is precious to Him. You are telling Him "I need You, I can't go on without You, only You can deal with this situation." 
- When the Son of Man comes will He find faith on the earth? We can pray back to Him, "Yes, Amen Lord, You will find faith on the earth, because You are our source." Our faith will be challenged before this. Whether it's real or shallow or deep. There seems to be no answer. "I still believe and I still have faith and I still am praying." After praying and praying, now I know you're touching me. Praise Him. You don't have to do anything, just praise. This is the way to engage in warfare. Christ has done it already, we praise Him. 

Linking faith - the faith that brings us into a life union with Christ, the faith that comes from contacting the Triune God continually. The faith joins us with the Triune God. The linking faith is the requirement to be an overcomer.

Linking faith is the faith of the believers who have no trust in themselves; rather, their trust is in God. (2Cor1:9)
