# Message Eight - Knowing and experiencing the all-inclusive Christ in Matthew

(James Lee)

We have been learning all the statuses and symbols of Christians, but we also must know the all-inclusive Christ. God brought us out of Egypt, but not only that, but into the good land, the all-inclusive Christ. This was the goal, the purpose, not merely salvation from bondage. That we would enjoy all the riches, build up the temple and the city as God's kingdom on the earth. The good land is the all-inclusive type of the all-inclusive Christ, with all its riches. Without these riches God's house, God's temple, can never be built up. We are not just believing in some kind of doctrine. Our believing has an object, a wonderful Person. We believe Him. All that He is. All that He has accomplished. This is the object of our belief. We must experience Him.

In 1962 brother Lee came to this country, the first conference was the "All-inclusive Christ". A Christian country like America, but probably not even 5/100 can tell you Christ is all-inclusive.  He has so many aspects, so many riches. After the conference, that was how the Lord's recovery began in this country. It opened a vast wealth of revelation to enjoy. Christ is so rich. For us to enjoy and experience.

In 2022 we completed the crystallisation study of the Bible. The Lord led the brothers to come back to consider the matter of the all-inclusive Christ. After that we spent four trainings on experiencing, enjoying and expressing Christ. What a Christ has been revealed! This is not a small thing. fourty-eight messages. I believe there is a burden on the Lord's heart, and He would not let us go. 

After 64 years since the opening of this vision. The knowledge and teaching of this matter is well established. But the Lord checked with me, how all-inclusive is He to me? In my experience. We know so much. We covered from Matthew to Revelation. I can't count how many aspects we touched. How much of this all-inclusive Christ has been revealed and made real in our being, in our living. I believe it's in the heart of the Triune God to bring us into the reality, into the experience, of this wonderful, all-inclusive Person. This is the only way the church as the Body of Christ can be built up, the Bride can be prepared. The gold and materials are the riches of the Triune God. How many riches do we have? I dare not say we don't have any. But we have to admit that we are still short. As I travel, visiting churches. I watch, I listen. Most of the times, same songs, repeating lines. Not much of the saints expressing their real appreciation of their enjoyment, in certain aspects, of their experience of this all-inclusive Christ. I have this conviction, Lord, I don't enjoy You enough. The all-inclusive Christ should not just be a book on a bookshelf. He has to be wrought into us, constituting our being. That there would be the riches of Christ when we gather together in our church life. In all the meetings we are not carrying out a formality. We are here to exhibit Christ. Even just one or two items that we enjoy. It will make a difference. It's not the amount you can recite. It's one or two of those items that have been worked into you, that you enjoy so much. When we gather together it would overflow from you.

I wonder, why Matthew? Why not Colossians, Ephesians? But I believe it's sovereign. As believers you must know Matthew. Many young believer read John and enjoyed it. But that is your preference. According to God's sovereign arrangement, the New Testament begins with Matthew. You say, "I don't like all those names." In God's arrangement, if you want to know Him, experience Him, you have to begin with Matthew. Matthew is the gospel of the kingdom. The main constitutent of the gospel is not life or grace but the kingdom. When Jesus came out to minister the first thing He said was, Repent! Repent for what? Repent for the kingdom! Believe it or not, Matthew is richer than the gospel of John. Matthew has 33 items of Christ. Richer than John in the revelation of the all-inclusive Christ. Tonight we picked up 14 points.

This revelation fills the entire New Testament. I hope as we get into this Matthew, many points may be familiar to you. You know them and have heard them before. But it's not about your having heard it or known it, but how much is it your experience? I really sense this is what the Spirit is after, is burdened for. To bring the saints to a richer experience of this all-inclusive Christ. Our life as a believer should be rich. It's one thing to be alive, but it's another thing to be rich. When the saints really enjoy the all inclusive Christ, when you come to the meeting, my, you sense the riches overflowing, in the prayers, in the singing, in the prophecying. The study questions are not about how much you know but how much have they become your experience?

As believers called out of Egypt we are now being called to the good land. Believers are receivers. God has done everything for us. We have to believe and receive all God has done and prepared for us. The first generation that came out of Egypt could not enter in because of their unbelief. We need to believe. God has called us for this good land. This is our destiny, to enjoy this all inclusive Christ. The first generation did not make it because of their heart of unbelief. I pray that by His mercy we will have a spirit of belief that God has called us to this good land. We are a people of faith, not merely disciples learning something and doing something. We are believers. God has done everything for us.

## I. Christ is the heavenly King.

Even from the first verse we are told He is the son of David and the son of Abraham. Although Abraham was earlier, David was mentioned first. Jesus as the King Saviour, He is the son of David, the king, and _then_ the son of Abraham. Abraham received the blessing of life. But David is the King with the Kingship. Under David as the King, then you can enjoy the blessing of the life dispensing of Abraham.

This King is altogether different from any kings we have on this earth. The kings of this earth have a certain kind of status and air. But when _this_ king was on His way to Jerusalem He came meek and mounted on a donkey. Here is the king of all kings, coming to the capital city, riding on a young donkey. Here is the king of the Jews. Will you be impressed by that? You think He will be one the horse of majesty with a royal gown. But He was just a lowly man, the son of a carpenter. He rode on a donkey. This is our King. Although He is lowly, He is meek, but He is kingly. Not in an outward way, to try to subdue and control people. He was a King. He was the One representing God's rule, having the highest authority, but He presented Himself in such a humble way.

This is one of the riches of the all inclusive Christ. A king in lowliness and meekness. Sometimes I observe when brothers become more achieving they exhibit a certain kind of air. That is not the pattern we see of Christ. We need to experience this Christ's kingliness in humility and meekness. There's a lot of feeling about this point. This is totally different from our natural concepts. Not to rule over others, to direct others.

## II. The Lord Jesus is the Christ, the Son of the living God

"Who do men say that I am?" You are the Christ, the Son of the living God. This is a great revelation. This lowly Jesus is the Christ, the Son of the living God.

The Christ - the anointing One to accomplish God's purpose. When you mention the word Christ, it implies He was the sent One, the anointed One, the commissioned One, passing through incarnation, crucifixion and resurrection, and the One coming again. Peter recognised, You are that One. Do you enjoy Him as _the_ Christ? As the anointed One? Christ is God's anointed One, and we the believers are also anointed and joined unto Him under the same anointing. As brothers who minister the Word we are very conscious of the need of the Lord's anointing. We don't want to just babble some words. Even in your preaching to your friends, to sinners, you need the anointing. Even as you function, prophecying, spherding new ones. You need to experience Him as the Christ anointing you.

The Son of the living God - His Person, embodying the Father and consummating in the Spirit. All the Father is is contained and embodied in Him.  "All the Father has is Mine." He is being realised as the Spirit for us to partake and enjoy. He is not just a God high up in the heavens. He is now the Spirit, approachable and realisable by us. Now you are sharing in all that this Triune God is.

This has to be real to us. We are not worshipping a God far away in the heavens. 

## III. Christ is the Son of Man

Many times the Lord took the position of a man. Even when tempted by Satan, Satan said "if you are the Son of God" but Jesus replied "_man_ shall not live by bread alone." In God's economy man is the means for God to carry out His purpose for His expression and dominion. God did not chose angels, He chose man. With His image and likeness. Man is so crucial for the carrying out of God's eternal purpose. When Jesus was incarnated, God became a man. Before John He went to be baptised. Jesus said, "to fulfill all righteousness". Even there, He was standing in the position of a man. As a man He needed to be baptised, terminated, before He could be qualified to carry out His ministry. Don't you enjoy His being a man? Also you are a man. Such a man lived such a life, went to the cross, was resurrected, ascended, still as a man. Satan was afraid of that man. He knew it would be because of a manchild, he would be put into the lake of fire. All the time he tried to address the Lord Jesus as the Son of God, but the Lord stood firmly on the position of a man.

He is not only humble and lowly. He is a victorious man! In him, the devil has nothing. No groud, no possibility. What a man! When He was on the cross all the evil angels tried to stop Him from going through the process of the cross. The evil people sneered and said come down from the cross and we will believe in you. You can save others you cannot even save yourself, what kind of God are you? But He remained silent. Apparantly so weak, so helpless, being put to an end. But He was victorious. He accomplished the wonderful redemption. He was a victorious man. 

Don't you enjoy this aspect of Christ? Don't you feel when you come to the table meeting you can proclaim, Lord Jesus, You are that man! You can hardly hear this expression among Christians today.

## IV. Christ is the Baptiser

John's baptism was a baptism of repentance. But "He who is coming after me is stronger than I ... He himself will baptise you in the Holy Spirit and fire."

What can I enjoy of Christ as the Baptiser? He is baptising people in the Holy Spirit. To baptise someone is to immerse that person. We have been put _into_ Christ. Unlike a mere ceremonial procedure, here Jesus is the real baptiser, baptising us in the Holy Spirit. With every baptism a new one passes through there should be such a realisation.

We have been put into Him. He and we have been joined, brought into an organic union. Now I am oganically joined to you. You have been put into the Triune God. You can claim, "Lord, I am in You. I have been put into You."

The baptism in the Holy Spirit initiates the kingdom of the heavens, the baptism of fire concludes the kingdom of the heavens, putting the unbelievers into the lake of fire.

## V. Christ is the light of life shining in the darkness of death

He didn't carry out much. He just called them. Come follow Me. As those working we should not have any trust in advertising, etc. We need to be the light of life. 

When brother Lee came to America he didn't come with a trumpet. He just came and prayed for three weeks. Then he held a conference. Something was shining out of this man, whose English was not even perfect at that time. But still, people were drawn. Christ is the light of life, scattering the darkness of death.

## VI. In calling people to follow Him for the kingdom, Christ ministed as a Physician and revealed Himself as the Bridegroom

Christ is our physician, healing us. Our body, soul and spirit. He is drawing us with His love. 

## VII. Christ is the unfulled cloth for making a new garment

When we come to the table meeting we should have appreciation that Christ is our new garment, our righeouesness. That I can stand righteously before God. These are the riches of Chrsit we should experience.

## VIII. The individual Christ is the new wine, and the corporate Christ is the fresh wineskin

Christ is the new wine, cheering us. 

You cannot put new wine in the old wineskin. Too much fermenting power. This new wineskin is the church life. It is the container for this exciting, invigorating Christ. I have been drining of this new wine for over 60 years. It is still cheering me up. But without this new wineskin there is no way for this new wine to be contained.

## IX. Christ is the Shepherd and the Lord of the harvest

As those serving and working for the Lord. This work, this harvest, belongs to Him. It does not belong to you. Lord, You are the Lord of the harvest. You thrust our labourers according to Your timing and Your way. Recently I was listening to reports from the move in Europe. The Lord was just sending ones here and there for the harvest. The same in Africa. From the time we first went to Africa we never asked one single person to go there. The Lord of the harvest sent them. We trust in what the Lord of the harvest can do.

## X. Christ is the Friend of sinners and the wisdom of God

We are not a friend of the world, that is to be at emnity with God. But be a friend of sinners.

We should not despise sinners. The Lord Jesus was a friend of sinners. He was so approachable. He had this compassion and love. Christ is also our wisdom, how to handle many situations.

## XI. Christ is the One who gives rest

To rest doesn't mean to take a vaccation. It means to do the Father's will. The Lord said my burden is easy, my yoke is light. The yoke is not just on you, its always with two animals. You are yoked with the Lord. 

## XII. Christ is the greater Jonah

The greatest sign - a group of God-men who live by the death and resurrection of Christ. 

## XIII. Christ is the bread and crumbs under the table

Jesus called her a dog. If I were her I would say forge about it. But this woman was full of faith. You've fallen onto the groud where I am. I'm a little dog, I can feed on those crumbs.

God's economy is not a matter of outward things but of Christ coming into us as food.

All these aspects are to be experienced by us, enjoyed by us.

## XIV. Christ in His humanity is the resurrected One with all authority in heaven and on earth

When we go out to preach the gospel, we go out with the One who has all authority. For discipling the nations.

## XV. The gospel of Matthew is full of the riches of Christ; we need to worhsip Him, enjoy Him, and experience Him as the wonderful, all-inclusive One

What riches will be displayed when the church comes together and we are not just doing the routine things but expressing all these riches.
