# Message Seven - Vessels of mercy unto honor and glory, grains of wheat, and branches of the vine

By our glorious regeneration we have a potential that is endless promises. We appreciate all these symbols (we are covering in this message), but how much have we matured? Our regeneration is our beginning, but in our living it requires our pursuit. 

The Lord is eager to come back, He is preparing us to be His bride. Maturity is the enlargement of our capacity. For the maturity of the bride the capacity needs to be enlarged. Our capacity to esteem Him, hold Him incomparable.

The Lord loves us all the time and we need to love Him all the time. In this way we will have an opening in every part of our being. It is impossible to grow in life if we do not love the Lord. Without love we can do nothing, with love we can do everything.

We want to consider through these symbols, how can we advance?

vessels of mercy, grains of wheat, branches of vine. We should ask ourselves with a message like this, is there a relationship among them? A new perspective of how we can live in oneness with the One we love.

## I. The believers in Christ are vessels of mercy unto honor and glory; we are containers of Christ as mercy, honor and glory.

"vessels" is a great matter in the Bible. Rom. vessels of mercy prepared *beforehand*. His consideration of this extended way back. First prepared. Then regenerated. Now we have honor. How much honor do we have in us? As the measure of the honorable God increases in us we become vessels of honor. A vessel is not an instrument, it is a container. The metaphor is inanimate, but our relationship with Him is personal.

### A.

In Gen 2:7, God formed man as a potter would make a vessel. He was Elohim in His creation of other things, but Jehovah in His creation of man. He uses the divine "fingers" and then breaths into the nostrils and man becomes a living soul. This implies an intimate interaction. 

Acts 9:15, Paul a chosen vessel to bear my name before the Gentiles and the sons of Israel. Paul had this encounter in which he became a vessel. He said, "who are you?" "I am Jesus, why are you persecuting Me?" Jesus, Me, vessel. We are vessels to contain Jesus as the special treasure to be part of the corporate Me.

### B. "open vessel"

distinguish between being an open or closed vessel. We don't intend to be a close vessel. Are you open? None of us would say we are not. What does it mean to be a vessel here?

"My real burden is to pin you down. Point out what your real trouble is. You love the Lord, the church. You may say you are open. But you're not open. You're open to your knowing, your doing. Your logic is, the One you love, you work for. The Biblical knowledge is, love the Lord, but do not do anything by yourself. We think, Oh, gotta do something. Natural doing, natural strength. The Lord wants you to love Him to the uttermost. He wants you to contact Him, touch Him, stop, and remain in that intimate touch."

to be open is to actively contact Him, be in an intrinsic touch, speaking to Him. Love the Lord, yet not do anything. Let the Lord to do everything. Then you are no longer the former you. You're the composite you, the vessel with the content. then what you do is out from Him with Him as the Person.

Are you open to Him now? Are you actively turning to Him? Set on Him? Not easily distracted from Him? This is to be a characteristic of us to be an open vessel. He has a free way to move out of our spirit, into our heart, into our being, because of our invitation to Him, actively. Our being filled is when we open to Him, contact Him, touch Him, give Him an opening to Him. Open to Him, contact Him, and "hold that pose as we live." Watch what He does in our daily living.

2 Kings 4:1-7, an impoverished woman who goes and borrow vessels, take them inside the house and shut to door. Pour the oil into the vessels you have borrowed. Once all the vessels were there the flowing continued. Once there were no more vessels the flowing stopped. While there is an openness in our being the flowing continues, but once there is no more open part the flowing stops. We need to direct our inner being toward Him. Render to Him a continually open vessel. 

"the hungry is filled and the rich are sent away empty".

We have two deficiencies that are the root of every problem we have: we don't know ourselves, and we don't know His riches. God is waiting for us to empty ourselves. If there is an infinite emptiness in us the Holy Spirit will grant us an infinite filling.

the following points. how to have this inertia out of complacency to touch Him.

### C. vessels of **mercy**

This is to be in awe of Him, His mercy.

"Your maker is your Husband." He prepared us as vessels of mercy with this in view. He knew us. A fore-knowing of what our fallen state would be. He saw us, appraised us, approved of us, possessed us, and married us. If we did not realise the depths of our fall, our appreciation of Him can't rise to what it needs to be for us to be open vessels. The economy of our appreciation of His preciousness requires our wretchedness.

we are here. this is His sovereign mercy. we can't explain it. but it should cause us to love Him, be moved by Him.

if we are an open vessel, treasuring Him, remaining in touch with Him, all these impatient parts of our being are inactivated and we are merciful to our neighbours, spontaneously. This begins every morning, realising His compassions are new every morning. 

### D. vessels of **honor**

vessels of honor are innoculations

Here we are in a stage and age of decline. Need of inoculators. Five statuses. These all have to do with being open vessels.

1. Teacher, a minister, nourished with words of life, healthy teachings, which keep us in the openness.

2. Soldier, waring against different teachings, fighting enemy against death by being full of life to reign in life. a spectrum of experiences: being equiped with the truth to recognise the errors, wars against the dissenters, fights against death, is filled in life, and reigns in life. to fight the good fight is to not allow the Lord's appearing to fail. he does it. we need to remain in the touch with Him.

3. Athlete, fleeing to Christ as the refuge, pursuing Christ as righteousness, faith, love, peace. Running in the lanes. Fleeing and pursuing. An athlete has to practice his skill. what's our skill?  Our skill is to be an open vessel. that alows us to always run within the lines, to run the race, complete the course, with those.

4. Farmer, sow the seed of life into people and water them with the Spirit and the words of life. We love His word, pursue Him in His Word. We hold that pose and work together with Him by being a vessel of honor. An aura, emanation from such a person. Emanate something registered by every man, a seed sown into them. We have our gospel presenation and words, but it's our holding this pose that give it the attraction, the penetrance, the potency. 

5. Workman, cutting straight the word of the truth, the main failure of the church was to consider this mainly in an outward way. the brethren continued in this way. they made this their focus. But it is to do this by living out these truths. If you live this way there is the demonstration of the lived out truth. This is what innoculates, enlightens, and brings others back. living this way enlightens people.  these truths are all for us to be under His economy of grace. we realise, it's okay to be simple. we don't become complicated. our concern is, how much we are able to enjoy, impart, what we enjoy. when we come to His word we come to a door, which may be closed. we have to open the door. that door has a latch or knob, on our side. we have to open the door and the light shines. we open the door by coming to Him, opening to Him, and being simple. He gives understanding to the simple.

### E. vessels of **glory**

God Himself is glory.

the treasure in us is the face of Jesus Christ, His presence, His Person. The glory is this face, engaged by us, loved by us, allowing us to be a vessel of glory. Don't you love this? What is your appreciation of the treasure? If we appreciate it, we don't want to neglect it, grieve it, turn away from it. We want to turn to it, engage it. we have the face of Jesus Christ as the treasure in us. Paul forgave us in the person (face) of Jesus Christ.

Do you think Paul (only) glanced at the Person of Chrsit when he needed some encouragement? If we treasure the treasure we will face the face. treasure and face are substitues. they are nouns but also verbs. "treasured up" do you treasure up the face? one skill we can have as athletes, is facing the face, in the complications of our daily life.His presence should be the "north pole" of the "compass" of our being. 

this treasuring of the face is our daily living, and this is our ministry. 2 cor 3, concerning the new testament ministry. the old testament minstry came in glory but faded, but the new testament ministry is in glory. it abouds in glory. ch5, brings us into the God of glory. (doesn't it also use the glory of aaron's face disappearing as an example)

as we behold we reflect. we beam, we shine forth. our heart receiving Him as honor and glory causes our heart to swell with joy.

 
## II. The believers in Christ are grains of wheat

consider these two symbols. how do we remain open vessels? We have these aspects, being innoculators, treasuring His face. But on the not so positive side, we stop ourselves. We contact Him and we stop. We keep our natural being from doing what it's trained to do. This is a picture of being a grain of wheat.

A. the grains are crushed, baked in the oven, blended with the oil into the meal offering. those grains that had a shell and a germ within, those shells had to be broken, crushed, mingled with oil then baked in an oven. 


B. The Lord is this One. He didn't just die at His death, but all through the gospel.

C. our natural being, natural capacity, natural flairs. we may think the decaying of the outer man is the age related deprecation of our body. but that's not the decay of the outer man. the outer man is not our body but our soul, with the body as the organ. for our outer man to be decaying means that the dynamic of being an open vessel prevails, the Lord becomes the Person. Our soul gets weaker, not longer, dominates, no longer leads. This happens spontaneously when we turn to Him and contact Him. 

D. read: the Bible, read the inward sense, read the environment and circumstances. if we don't read the inward sense the environment may "remind" us, we need to come back.

E. as pillars in the temple we have in the capital a complicated network over the pomegranates, which is a complicated environment that we don't try to escape from. Pass from being prisoners *of* Christ to being prisoners *in* Christ, which is to recognise Him in the environment and invite Him to come in and be with us.

F., G.

## III. The believers in Christ are branches of the vine

As vessels, we are open. As grains of wheat we fall into the groud to not live by our natural life. Then we are free to abide in Him as branches in the vine. Live in and with the Divine Trinity.

A, B

C. Abide in His love. Express Him in fruit bearing.

D. Daily, hourly, minutely.

E. Abide in His words and His words will abide in us.

F. Live, grow, express, propogate Christ in everyhing. What is it to work? The fruit of our work is to be in the full knowledge of Him.

G. Fruit bearing the overflow of the inner life.

A few things that will help you to abide:
- realise you are already in Him (the rest of our being needs to join Him there)
- our abiding in Him is unbroken fellowship with Him.
