## I. Disciples - following and learning Christ, experiencing Christ in His experiences

A disciple is a learner. A follower. One who learns by following.

Matt. 5:1. He spoke to His disciples, His followers, His learners. His inner circle. His audience to release the crucial word in Matt 5-7. endevour to be the Lord's best audience to release what's on His heart.

Haggai 2:23, "I will make you as a signet" God considered him a representative He had chosen. A signet had an offical seal, something worn by the king. All disciples are the Lord's signet. Representing Him. Always in His hand. You represent Him in oneness with Him.

Matt. 28:19, "disciple all the nations" - that everyone we preach to would become a disciple. we like to share with people, we like them to receive, but theLord wants to disciple them. Baptising them is the first step. When the Lord found some by the sea He said, "Come after Me." Immediately they left the nets, boat, father, and followed Him.

### A learn Christ

Discipled from being a natural man to being a God-man. Learn not to be a natural man. His discipling is to train us, to teach us, not to be natural man. Eph. 4, learning Christ as the reality is in Jesus. "the actual condition of the Lord Jesus as recorded in the four gospels" The Lord's living in the gospels is a reality. In His daily life there was reality. When we go there and just read the gospels it has such an effect. In the past we took this as, we need to imitate Jesus. This never works. That reality of Jesus is like a mold. We were put into that mold in our baptism. Then He comes as the Spirit to live in us that life that He lived before. That's how we become His duplication. Our baptism has much significance. Our living is a living out of the reality of our baptism. Our baptism is the putting to death of the natural man.

Renewed in the spirit of our mind by the Word of God. For learning the mind is always engaged. We need the renewing of the mind. That change has to do with how we handle the word of God.

Deut 17:18-20, the law for the king. "he shall write out for himself a copy of this law in a book" - the first assignment for a king - to write out the Bible. "it shall be with him". the king should have his Bible. "he shall read in it" not just carry. read it. "all the days of his life". The king's first assignment was to read the Bible daily. We don't know any king that actually did this. "that he may learn to fear Jehovah his God". the king should be the first learner. Do we want to take the lead? even in your group meeting. even in shepherding young people. the first thing is to learn, and continue your learning every day of your life. Learn to fear Jehovah. Fear Jehovah will be a safe guard for our humanity.

### B. learn Christ by eating Jesus

The Lord had a supply, the "curds and the honey". curds, the richest grace. honey, the sweetest love. The Lord Himself was under a divine supply. "Father, the love with which You loved Me I have given them." the Lord was there all the time being loved by the Father. Now we get to be graced in the Beloved. His life was one of eating. Our Christian life should be a life of eating. By eating we are discipled.

As a son the Lord learned obedience through the things He suffered. When we eat Him we can eat His obedience. Isn't it wonderful we can eat Him?

We need the power, firstly to refuse, and then the power, to chose. That only can come by our eating.

The love of God has been poured out in our hearts. Thank You God. The love of God has been poured out into my heart. Say it. When you say it, you have it. You already had it. When you say it you enter into the reality.
