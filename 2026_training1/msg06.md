# Message Six - Brothers of Christ as the Firstborn of God

we have a certain status. stone and sheep are certain status. being brothers of Christ is also related to our status.

We have a status, but that status is enabled by something the Lord experiences. Being the firstborn means He is not the only one. There was an underlying process the Lord went through that enables us to have a certain status. This is something we ourselves could never work out. The Christian life is not a life of trying to be a better person. The Christian life is a life of having the same status as the Firstborn Son. God's intention is to produce many other sons to follow the Firstborn.

This message is kind of like a puzzle. At the beginning you don't know what it is. You have to put pieces together for a picture to emerge.

From eternity Christ was the only-begotten Son. As such He is unique. However in resurrection the only-begotten became the first-born. He needed to be begotten in resurrection because He had put on humanity. His human nature was uplifted and transfigured into a glorious substance.

## I. "I will raise up your seed... I will be his Father, and he will be My Son" (2 Sam. 7:12a, 14a)

David had a desire to do something for God, to build Him a house, but God said no, I will do something for you. In a literal sense this was Solomon, but actually Christ is the son of David. The geneology in Matthew shows this. But according to God's evaluation He is God's Son. What a wonderful Person our Christ is.

We are a unique kind of people. We are brothers of Christ. And He is not ashamed to call us brothers. God has done something marvelous to include us.

2 Samuel 7 is matched and expounded by Romans 1:3-4. He is designated the Son of God in power according to the Spirit of holiness. To understand the Bible, the Bible itself interprets the Bible. We are not relying on traditional church doctrines. What we are relying on is the Bible itself. When consider 2 Samuel 7 you need Romans 1. Christ a descendent of David has been designated the Son of God. This is the mystery of God becoming man to make man God in life and nature but not in the God-head. In Christ we can see clearly the God-man, divinity and humanity. But our Lord goes through a process. Heb. 1:5 says David's seed is Christ as God's first-born Son. "Your seed" and "My Son" implies a human seed becomes a divine Son.

2 Sam 7 - typology. Rom 1, explanation. Rom 8, He might be the first-born among many brothers. In His resurrection He is desginated the Son of God. What is designated? His humanity. He is already the Son of God in His divinity. In His humanity before the death and resurrection God had taken on the human life which had not yet entered into divinity. In His resurrection He becomes the First-born Son of God.

Matt. 22:41-45. Shortly before the Lord is going to be crucified, there is a sequence of events, pharisees, saducees, chief priests, questioning him. The lawyer asks, what is the law? The Lord deals with all the questions. Now it's the Lord's turn and He asks them a question. The question of questions. You want to talk about politics, religion, the law. Now I have the question of questions. "Whose son is He?' David's. "How then does David in spirit call Him Lord?" "If then David calls Him Lord, how is He his son?" Who is Christ. A question every man needs to deal with. Who is Christ? He is the son of man. He is the Son of God. What a Christ we have.

Don't forget the phrase, "but not in the God-head". We are not and never will be the same as Christ in His Diety, His Headship, His being Lord of all.

## II. In Acts 13 Pauls says that from David's seed, "God, according to promise, brought to Israel a Savior, Jesus", and that "God has fully fulfilled this promise..in raising up Jesus, as it is also written in the second Psalm, "You are my Son; this day have I begotten You."

Resurrection was a birth to the man Jesus. This birth is a birth in His humanity. His humanity is being designated, brought into God. 

The only-begotten from eternity. John 1:18, "no one has even seen God, the only begotten Son in the bossom of the Father, He has declared Him."

The begetting is the day of resurrection, and that begetting produces the First-born Son. In a real sense, the First-born did not exist before the day of resurrection.

Two births. John 1:14, puts on humanity. At this point He is a God-man. Rom. 8:29, in resurrection He becomes the First-born. Before incarnation the only begotten Son did not have humanity. After His resurrection the First-born Son has both the human nature and the divine nature.

1 Pet. 1:3, "regenerated us.. through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead." All of us were born again at the time of the Lord's resurrection. 

Among the many sons only the First-born is also the only-begotten. 

We have both divinity and humanity just like Christ the First-born Son.

III is a process Christ goes through, IV a process we go through.

## III. The seed of David becoming the Son of God speaks of the process of Christ's being designated the firstborn Son of God by resurrection.

The gospel of God is a gospel of sonship. The gospel concerns God's Son. 

God put on flesh, the human nature which had nothing to do with divinity. In His humanity He was not the Son of God. Of course in His divinity He was. He many times refered to Himself as the Son of Man.

The crucifixion is a key part of this process. Heb 2:14, "He might destroy him who has the might of death, that is the death". Brass serpent. By looking at the bronze serpent the children of Israel were saved from the poison of the serpents. Christ is the reality of the bronze serpent. **Our looking away to Jesus saves us from the poison of the serpent.** If you saw Christ you saw a human being. He bore flesh. He lived the life of a man. He entered into humanity. That humanity needed to be crucified because He was representing all of us. All of the natural man was put on the cross. The crucifixion eliminated the problems related to humanity. That paved the way for the activity of His resurrection to produce Him as the firstborn Son.

The step now is He can become the Son of God in His humanity.

"sonized" means to be made a son, but what is it to be "designated". In Jesus the human part is related to the negative things of the flesh. How could this human part be related to His divine part. In His resurrection the human part was uplifted to the same level as the divine part.

When a seed dies in the soul the operation of the seed's life is activated. The death is what makes way for Christ to be designated, glorified and resurrected. The Spirit of holiness operated in His death and in resurrection He "blossomed" as the Son of God.

## IV. The seed of David becoming the Son of God speaks of the process of our being designated the many sons of God by resurrection

Heb. 2:10-11, leading many sons into glory... He who sanctifies and those who are being sanctified are all of one... for which cause He is not ashamed to call them brothers.

We are all of One. We are all of the One Father.

The outer man is decaying but the inner man is being renewed. Hallelujah for our inner man. This is what matters.

I hope this message would cause us, aspire us for the growth in life. Identify as a son of God. 

- In order to grow we need a heart turned to the Lord and a heart pure toward Him. 
- We need to feed on the guileless milk and the solid food of the word. Not the husks of the world. You can be fully occupied with social media. This can become your diet. The more we feed on the milk and the food, we help intensify the process of growth.
- We need the watering of the gifted members; join ourselves to vital members, influencing one another to grow.
- Through our environment our ugly self is torn down and the Lord has the way to work within us. This is very much a part of the process. The Lord is not ashamed to call us brothers, despite our failures. Our environment is used to tear down our ugly self.

One day this process will be completed. Hallelujah we are on the way. We are in the process, but there is a time for completion. "Beloved now we are children of God and it not yet been manifested what we will be. We know that if He is manifested we will be like Him, because we will see Him even as he is." (1 John 3:2) The inevitable result is we will be the same as He is.

We are being saved from a self likeness. As I spent time with the Lord the light came and I realised, all I care about is my self. All that matters is how people treat me. We are being delivered from self likeness, to bear the appearance of brothers of Christ. He has become us in order to make us Him.

The key is resurrection. Christ is the resurrection. We need to be those who cooperate with Him to let Him live out through us. We urgently need to learn how to walk according to the spirit. It really is an urgent need to learn how to walk according to the spirit. Not to treat the spirit lightly. To pay attention to the inner life and peace, and in this way cooperate with the process. The more we touch the Spirit the more we are sanctified.

The New Jerusalem is ultimately a matter of constitution--God and man.

## V. God's purpose is to produce many brothers of His firstborn Son for His expression

We have the same Father. It is a wonderful fact that the Firstborn has many brothers. Hallelujah for all the brothers! "Go to my brothers and tell them, I ascend to My Father and your Father, my God and your God." Our connection with our brother, Christ, is that we have the same Father. The same life and nature as He does. We have been born of God. Now we share the same life, the same nature, because we come from the same source. We are qualiied to call ourselves brothers of Christ and sons of God.

We have not only a creating God but a begetting Father. 

"He is not ashamed". Sometimes we are ashamed about ourselves. We have the Lord's blood and forgiveness. But as far as He is concerned, He is not ashamed. Isn't that sweet. We may make mistakes and fail but He is not ashamed of us. 

"I will declare Your name to my brothers." He's imparting the Father into us. "In the midst of the church I will sing hymns of praise to You." Who is singing, us or the Lord? Yes. In our singing He sings hymns of praise to the Father. We can develop a sweet and intimate relationship with God as our Father. How thankful we are that He has predestinated us, chosen us, to bring us into this sonship.

When we talk about the building up of the church or the Body, this building is the constitution of the divine element into the human element and the human element into the divine element to make a mutual constitution. Our work must be related to this mutual constitution.
