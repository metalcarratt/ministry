# Message Twelve - The Bridegroom, the maturity of the Bride, and being a warrior to defeat God's enemy

## I. Christ is the Bridegroom

The most pleasant Person. Four ways to enjoy Him as the Bridegroom: as a companion in the bridechamber; as guest at His wedding feast; as virgins; as the Bride.

I don't believe the Bridegroom will say at that time, "Nice to meet you." (as if meeting for the first time). At that time it will be as two lovers rushing to embrace each other. 

## II. We need to see and understand the maturity of the bride

Maturity. From the spiritual side, what is maturity? *The Experience of Life*, the need to pass through the third stage. It's when we pass through the third stage, with the cross applying to our self, disposition, and other aspects, thoroughly touching us, something will take place inwardly. When we enter the fourth stage, the stage of authority and warfare, your spirit will give you the realisation, you not only have an independent spirit, but your spirit is a spirit of the Body. To many of us "the Body" is only a word. This is a matter in the fourth stage. When you realise you're not independent. At this stage you've reached maturity, spiritually speaking. Many dear saints have been in the Lord's recovery and the church meetings for decades, but when they passed away they had not passed through the third stage. I'm mentioning this with much loving care. The Lord is giving us the opportunity to grow to maturity. At the very end we may find the seven Spirits intensifying what is taking place in our experience.

None of you will marry someone who is immature. Saints may be saying, Come, Lord, Come. He may say, "I'll come when you're mature. I'm not coming for a little girl, I'm coming for my wife, who has to be parallel to Me in maturity." 

How did the wife "make herself ready"? It's not just God making us ready. We have to know how to make ourselves ready. The sister who got married made all kinds of arrangements ahead of time. Making ourselves ready by being mature. And when we are ready we will be ready for spiritual warfare.

Early in the morning my wife and I pray, Lord we want to experience a particular aspect of You today. Every day is a Christ-experience-and-enjoy-day. Just pray, Lord today I want to experience You in a particular way. Even if you have never done this, if you start now and do this to the end of the year, there will be a marvelous deposit of Christ in your being.

Maturity is to be filled with the divine life. Nothing else filling you up. We are not empty, we cannot exist as a human being. But we want to be filled with the divine life. In our fellowship with Him we have to be open. He may say, "I'm removing this from you. I'm going deeper now. This is just the self, even something demonic. I remove it, by the cross. And I dispense Myself into you." We are not empty, otherwise we wouldn't be alive very long. But when we are open to the Lord, the time will come when you're not holding anything back, and the Lord will go to the depths of your being, deeper than you yourself know exists. We don't try to examine ourself. Just be an open vessel and allow Him to go to the depths of your soul and allow Him to replace it with the all-inclusive Christ. Probably brother Watchman Nee was the most profoud Christian since the early apostles. If you read some of his books from the 1930s, you can see the depth of his writing. It encourages you to realise, no doubt he was a genius, for example he could just remember everything. But when he was in prison for 20 years he said at the end, my joy continued. He was faithful to the end. That is the "end point".

This is not just theology. This is what's in the Lord's heart for all of you. Don't be afraid. Just trust God in His wisdom and His love. He knows where we are, He knows the next step, He knows what we need to go through. He will just lead you and guide you to the goal. He wants you to be ready for His coming. He's not coming for a child, he's coming for a spiritual woman to be His wife.

A mature believer **knows** and **cares** for the Body of Christ, being **Body-conscious** and **Body-centered**. Dear brothers, especially middle-aged brothers. I hear you every week, "the Body, the Body." You know the meaning of the word, but I have to be honest with you. You're not in the experience of the Body yet. Just allow the Lord to show you where you are in the stage of life. Then you'll be able to say, "I just knew about the Body, but now I'm part of the Body. I cannot live without the Body. I'm living and going through all kinds of things for the Body." This is the goal of God's perfect will. Eph. 4:16.

Older saints, it's very serious to be "content". I've met some in conversation. Their feeling is, I'm content, I've passed through everything. I'm ready for the Lord. They don't realise they haven't passed through the third stage. And I can't say anything to you because you're not willing to know where you really are. After talking with you for a minute or thirty seconds, I know where you are. Not to judge you. We may not know where we are fully, but we come to the Lord and pray, Lord I'm open to You. I used to wake up early and go walk around the park, one day I prayed, "I need Your help. You can see me, but I can't see You. How can I have fellowship with You?" That was the beginning. He said, "Yes, you won't see me for a while. But you know I'm on the throne, I'm in the meetings, I'm in your spirit. Just keep exercising your spirit, and I will keep dispensing Myself into you, little by little, for you to grow unto maturity."

A lot takes placed between 12 and 19. It's not a lengthy time. It's somewhat parallel to passing through the third time.

James 5:7, the farmer eagerly awaiting with long-suffering the precious fruit of the earth. The Lord Jesus is the real farmer. He is awaiting with patience our maturity in life. Only our maturity can hasten His coming back. His feeling right now is, He is awaiting our maturity. He's been willing to wait for the rest of your life, and for the large part of the next thousand years. But why not make Him happy by becoming mature here and now. Then He doesn't need to wait the coming age. If we are serious about waiting for the Lord's coming back, we need to grow in life unto maturity. I hope all of us will realise this. This is serious. This is deep within me. I can't wait to see you. These two matters need to be put together in your own being. "Lord, I want to see You, but I know I need to grow in life. And day by day I will pray-read the Word and having a relationship with You and open my being to You." Even for a brother of sister 13 years old, it's not too soon to grow in life. We just encourage and help you to have a personal relationship with the Lord day by day, and pray-read the Word. And you will see what will take place in the core of your being. Things that can seem to be real, you can get right away. But when something is real because of the life, it takes time. That's a normal thing. He's giving time for everyone of you to grow in life unto maturity and be ready for His coming. No matter who you are and what you've gone through. It's not too soon. And for those who are more than my age, it's not too late. The Lord will give you the time, and if the time is limited, He will give you the sevenfold Spirit to do in a month what would normally take three years.

## III. The church as the bride of Christ must be a warrior to defeat God's enemy

What is this particular aspect of the church? The church *must be* a warrior. You have been trained, in various different ways, for warfare. A few years ago some brothers asked me if every month for a few hours, give us training on spiritual warfare. I felt I would give that to the Lord. The churches and the saints in Europe realise what their situation is, because at a certain time the evil one will be manifested in that part of the earth. That's why we need to be a warrior.

### A. Col. 2:15, the warfare at Christ's crucifixion. The cross was the center of the universe.

Christ was on the cross for six hours. For the first three people walked by, mocking Him. But in the second three, every negative thing was placed on Him to die on the cross for our salvation. But at the same time God the Father was there, because all the demonic and evil things realised they were being defeated on the cross. It was invisible but it was real, there was a fighting taking place.

### B. purpose - bring in the kingdom

This is why we are fighting. Satan's kingdom is on the earth. But after the first 3.5 years the overcoming saints will be raptured to the heavens, and when they arrive there will be a war between angels. God's angels will defeat Satan and his angels, and all the evil powers will be thrown down to the earth. Right now, thousands of Christians and many hundreds in the Lord's recovery, will be on the earth in that 3.5 years because they were not mature. This will be the worst time of humanity on the earth. Will all the evil things all over the earth. We have to realise the fact. Right now we are learning about it. The Lord in His wisdom and love allows those second 3.5 years for the saints to become mature. The Lord in a secret way, with His bride, will be coming. The enemy will be terrified when he sees you. Sisters, don't think you're on a different level to the brothers when it comes to spiritual warfare.

Seek first the kingdom of God. In our daily living.

Where we are going to go when we get married. Some kind of beautiful country? Well, we are going to Armageddon. One reason I married you is you are trained to fight against evil things.

### E. Song of Songs - the lover becomes as terrible as an army with banners

I suggest, if you are under 30, read the book Song of Songs once a month. And get Watchman Nee's book on this. This is one of the most important books (in the Bible).

1. a. The lover of Christ is beautiful and comely before the Lord, as solid as the heavenly city and as serene as the santuary; at the same time, she displays the glory of her victory before the enemy and the world.

1. b. The overcoming lover of Christ not only has a future that is full of hope and a life that is absolutely heavenly, but she is also a victor who constantly triumphs in her victory.

2. b. Satan is not afraid of individualistic Christsians, even if they number in the thousands, but is terrified of the church as the Body of Christ, the corporate warrior fighting against him and his kingdom.

### F. David the warring Christ, Abigail the warring church

1. From 1 Samuel 25 onward, Abigail was always at the side of David the warrior and followed him in his wars.

spiritual warfare - Satan's will set against God's will. And then there's our will. The will of God is the source of everything that has been created. That's the foundation. (Rev). The will of God is for Christ to be everything to us (Col). The will of God is to build up the Body of Christ (Eph). I studied this by finding so many books about a person's will, "Lord, what is your will for me?" It's selfish. But God's will is we enjoy Christ, build up the church. Once we give ourselves for this, then you can say, "Now I need to know what is your will for *me*? I don't know any other way to live. I only want Your perfect will. Not something that you will allow." I left every denomination and left everything in 1986 (?) for this. I exist for Christ and the church.
